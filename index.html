❓ How to play

⚔️ Draw Your Sword trains you to know and find God's Word.

  • 🧠 Bible Quiz — answer questions from any part of the Bible.
  • ⚔️ Sword Drill — a verse is called; race to tap the right book.
  • ✍️ Finish the Verse — choose the right ending and hide God's Word in your heart.
  • 🔢 Books in Order — tap the books in their Bible order.
  • 🏕️ Campaign — journey section by section through the whole Bible.

Choose a level for each player — 🐣 Recruit (kids), 🛡️ Soldier (tweens/teens), 👑 Champion (adults). Everything works offline and the reference is always shown so you can look it up in your own Bible.
