🎙️ How hands-free works

  • Tap Start once and allow the microphone. After that, no tapping is needed.
  • It talks to you and then listens. When you see the green 🎤, speak.
  • Spell your name letter by letter, then say “done.” It reads your name back — say “yes” to keep it or “again” to re-spell.
  • Pick a color by saying it: “gold”, “blue”, “green”…
  • Answer a question by saying the number (“one”, “two”…) or the answer word. Say “repeat” to hear it again, or “pass” to skip.
  • Passing to the next player? Just say “ready.”

📱 Works best in Chrome (Android) or real Safari (iPhone) — not inside WhatsApp/Instagram, where the mic is blocked. If it ever mishears, every step still has a button you can tap.
